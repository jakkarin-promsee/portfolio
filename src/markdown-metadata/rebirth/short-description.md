# Rebirth

Categories: [game development, 2D action, Unity]

Summary: A playable pixel-art action game about escaping a prison, surviving room-based combat, and facing the Spirit King. Players dash, manually aim and swap among seven weapons while enemies patrol, pursue, circle, reposition, and dodge according to tunable state-driven behavior. Combat builds toward a telegraphed multi-phase boss fight and an ending affected by a narrative choice.

Highlights:
- Seven weapons covering melee reflection, guns, spread fire, piercing magic, arrows, rockets, and burn effects.
- Five regular enemy archetypes share a configurable movement and attack controller.
- Tilemap rooms close, spawn waves on valid floor tiles, and reopen after clearing; treasure rooms reward weapons.
- Spirit King boss uses 2D navigation, six special skills, HP-dependent phases, and visual attack warnings.

Tech stack: [Unity 2022, C#, Unity 2D, ScriptableObjects, tilemaps, NavMeshPlus, Universal Render Pipeline]
