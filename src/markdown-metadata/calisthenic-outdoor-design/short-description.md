# Outdoor Calisthenics Gym

Categories: [3D design, engineering research, physical product design]

Summary: A draft design and research package for a permanent backyard calisthenics gym shared by an adult and a young child. It combines Blender models of four steel exercise structures with dimensions, material choices, preliminary load and foundation checks, sourcing research, bilingual handover documents, and a proposed construction workflow. The work identifies points a licensed engineer must review before anything is built or used; it is not a certified structural design.

Highlights:
- Models a pull-up bar, snake bar, dip bars, a five-zone monkey/flying bar, and impact-absorbing flooring for a roughly 6 × 5 m plot.
- Organizes English and Thai design, verification, research, and handover documents for different readers.
- Flags critical structural and safety questions, including the monkey-bar brace, cantilevered grips, welds, anchors, and fall-surface thickness.
- Explores shop-welded, hot-dip-galvanized subassemblies joined by field bolts.

Tech stack: [Blender, Markdown, engineering calculations, EN 16630 and EN 1176 research]
